Proposed ICLR 2027 Workshop

AI4AR

AI for Aging Research

Learning, predicting, and intervening on biological aging.

Status: Workshop proposal for ICLR 2027. Acceptance notification expected November 29, 2026.

About

Aging is the greatest risk factor for most chronic diseases, yet AI cannot yet reliably measure how biological systems age, predict how they respond to interventions, or design interventions that slow or reverse aging.

AI4AR brings together AI researchers, computational biologists, and aging scientists around those three problems: measuring biological age and state, predicting responses to intervention, and designing the interventions themselves.

To our knowledge, this is the first workshop dedicated to AI for aging at a major AI conference.

Topics

Areas of interest

  • Representations of biological age and state
  • Aging clocks and biomarkers
  • Virtual cells and biological world models
  • Aging trajectories and intervention prediction
  • Causal inference and experimental design
  • Generative design of rejuvenation interventions
  • Multimodal and longitudinal biological data
  • Benchmarks, evaluation, and reproducibility

Call for Papers

Contributions

Submission details will be posted here if the workshop is accepted. We plan to use OpenReview, with non-archival papers. Work already published at a major ML venue, or at the ICLR 2027 main conference, will not be accepted.

Two tracks are planned: full papers (4–8 pages) and tiny papers (1–2 pages). Accepted papers will be posters. Selected full papers will be spotlight talks, and selected tiny papers will be short pitches.

The planned submission deadline is February 1, 2027 (11:59pm AoE), with author notification no later than February 26, 2027.
