Proposed ICLR 2027 Workshop

AI4AR

AI for Aging Research

Learning, predicting, and intervening on biological aging.

Status: Workshop proposal for ICLR 2027. Acceptance notification expected November 29, 2026.

About

Aging is the greatest risk factor for most chronic diseases, yet AI cannot yet reliably measure how biological systems age, predict how they respond to interventions, or design interventions that slow or reverse aging. AI4AR brings together AI researchers, computational biologists, and aging scientists to turn these three problems into well-posed machine learning challenges with shared benchmarks.

To our knowledge, this is the first workshop dedicated to AI for aging at a major AI conference. The one-day program combines keynotes, a panel, contributed spotlights, tiny-paper pitches, poster sessions, and an open-problems roundtable whose output will be a public AI4AR Open Problems and Benchmarking Roadmap.

The workshop is organized around three capabilities current AI systems still lack:

  • Measure. Learn representations of biological age and state, from cell to organism, that capture function, resilience, and responsiveness rather than only chronological age.
  • Predict and simulate. Infer aging trajectories from sparse data and predict the effects of genetic, chemical, and reprogramming interventions, including ones never seen in training.
  • Design. Propose new interventions and choose the next experiment, closing the loop between models and the lab.

Topics

Areas of interest

Submissions should have a substantive machine learning component. Topics follow the measure, predict, and design arc.

Measure: biological age and state

  • Representation learning for biological state, biological age, and aging trajectories
  • Aging clocks from single cells to tissues, organs, and clinical biomarkers
  • Multimodal integration across transcriptomic, epigenetic, proteomic, morphological, and spatial readouts
  • Cross-species and cross-tissue transfer of aging representations

Predict and simulate: virtual aging cells and world models

  • Virtual cells in time, and biological world models from cell to organism
  • Dynamical models and trajectory inference from cross-sectional and sparse longitudinal data
  • Counterfactual prediction of genetic, chemical, environmental, and reprogramming interventions
  • Causal representation learning for aging, and uncertainty under distribution shift

Design: discovering and optimizing interventions

  • Generative design and functional genomics for rejuvenation factors and targets
  • Optimizing combinations, dose, timing, and delivery
  • Active learning, experimental design, and lab-in-the-loop agents
  • Benchmarks and evaluation when lifespan and healthspan are hard to observe

Call for Papers

Two tracks

If the workshop is accepted, submissions will be through OpenReview on the ICLR workshop template. Both tracks are non-archival and double-blind. Papers already published at an ML conference, including the ICLR 2027 main track, are not eligible.

  • Full papers (4–8 pages, excluding references): original methods, empirical studies, benchmarks, or datasets. Eight spotlight talks.
  • Tiny papers (1–2 pages, excluding references): early ideas, negative results, re-analyses, perspectives, and new datasets. Six pitches.

Every submission must include an aging-relevance statement of at most 100 words: what the work teaches us about learning, predicting, or intervening on biological aging. The planned submission deadline is February 1, 2027 (11:59pm AoE), with author notification no later than February 26, 2027.
